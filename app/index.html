Three executed contracts are seeded — Baby Shower (Aug 8, 50 guests, $1,500 F&B minimum), the University of Maryland Inclusive Excellence retreat (Aug 19, 7 guests, $800 rental, tax-exempt), and the Gallin-Munson post-wedding breakfast (Aug 23, 65 guests, 15% military discount) — use the tabs to switch events, New Event to price a fresh inquiry from the house defaults, or Duplicate to start from the event on screen. Per the Tripleseat bills, the service charge runs on F&B only (never rental or other fees), the special event fee runs on F&B + rental, sales tax runs on food + special event fee (zeroed for exempt clients), and discounts come off F&B after fees. Every white cell is live in edit mode: change guests, menu quantities, prices, per-line COGS, staffing or rates and the client bill and theoretical P&L re-derive instantly; Save writes the event to the shared workspace with a change log entry, and if a teammate saved the same event while you were editing you'll be asked before anything is overwritten. The theoretical P&L counts everything the house keeps (F&B, cash-bar estimate, service charge, special event fee, rental) and excludes pass-through taxes; costs run per-line vendor unit costs, the staffing schedule with payroll burden (house rule: 2 servers + 1 bartender at $35/hr for a 4-hour event on a 5-hour shift, scaled to the event), the Vessel driver opex percentages (23.4% of event revenue) and the 4% management fee; no rent is charged to events. EBITDA here is event-level and theoretical — rent (waived from Aug ’26), allocated overhead and the replacement reserve sit outside the per-event view.
