## 2. Revenue basis

Tripleseat's `Event Grand Total` decomposes cleanly — verified on 221 of 236 priced
rows to the dollar:

```
Event Grand Total = Event Actual + Sales Tax + Gratuity + Admin Fee + Service Charge
```

The reporting basis used throughout this file:

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Event Grand Total | $4,549,983 |
| less Sales Tax | ($181,981) |
| less Gratuity | ($4,801) |
| **= Net revenue** | **$4,363,202** |

**Service charge ($618,280) and admin fee ($158,014) are retained** as house revenue.
Liquor tax is zero for this location. Gratuity is recorded on only 2 of 365 rows —
service charge is the operative uplift here, not gratuity.

### Multi-outlet attribution (the 80/20 rule)

An Anthology event that also uses another Book Tower outlet's space is reported
**80% Anthology / 20% the other outlet**. This applies at the event row level.

| Outlet | Shared event rows | 20% share |
|---|---|---|
| Kamper's | 42 | $359,678 |
| Bar Rotunda | 8 | $55,755 |
| **Total to other outlets** | **50** | **$415,432** |

**Le Suprême and HIROKI-SAN are excluded entirely** — they run their own book and
collect their own revenue. They never appear in an Anthology booking. See §7 for why
this matters.

## 7. Method — and two traps in this data

### The booking is the unit, not the event row

A Tripleseat booking holds every session belonging to one sale. A wedding booking
typically carries a **TASTING** (3–4 guests, months earlier), a **PHOTOGRAPHY** session,
a ceremony rehearsal and the wedding itself. Counting event rows inflates weddings from
81 to 201.

**Trap 1 — dating.** Anchoring a booking on its *earliest* event dates a May wedding to
January, when the tasting happened. Uncorrected this produced 25 "weddings" in a Detroit
January. Every booking here is anchored on its **highest-guest-count event**.

**Trap 2 — money repeats.** Booking-level fields (`Booking Minimum`) repeat verbatim on
every event row in the booking. Summing them across rows double-counts. Revenue here is
summed from **event-level** `Event Grand Total`, which does not repeat.

### Location 22266 is not just Anthology

The Tripleseat location holds two different books:

1. **Anthology's own events** — 13th Floor Event Space, Linden Room, Terrace Club,
   Conservatory Ballroom, Green Room, plus Kamper's Rooftop and Bar Rotunda *when used
   as part of an Anthology event*
2. **The restaurants' private dining** — Le Suprême PDR, Hiroki-San PDR, and standalone
   Kamper's / Bar Rotunda reservations

**This file covers only the first.** The export is scoped to Anthology's book; a raw API
pull of location 22266 returns both and overstates the business by ~134 bookings (Le
Suprême PDR 64, Hiroki-San PDR 20, standalone Kamper's 16, standalone Bar Rotunda 12).
Any analysis drawn straight from `/v1/events/search.json` for this location will be wrong
unless filtered by room.

Note also that **`/v1/events/search.json` silently ignores its `location_id` parameter**
and returns events across every venue on the account. Venue must be read from each
event's own `location.id`.

## 10. Known limitations

1. **Snapshot age.** The export's newest booking was created 13 Aug 2026. Business sold
   between then and 31 Aug is absent (approximately 4 bookings).
2. **Definite + Closed only.** Lost, Prospect and Tentative business is not in this file,
   so nothing here speaks to conversion rates or lost revenue.
3. **Covers are venue-recorded** expected/guaranteed counts, not verified door counts.
4. **Three weddings carry no priced revenue**, so averages rest on 78 of 81.
5. **Social is contaminated by photography sessions** — see §6 before using that segment.
6. **The 80/20 split is applied uniformly** to any event row using another outlet's room.
   If the actual commercial split varies by event, these attributions will differ.
7. **Service charge and admin fee are treated as house revenue.** If either is
   distributed to staff, net revenue overstates what the venue retains.
