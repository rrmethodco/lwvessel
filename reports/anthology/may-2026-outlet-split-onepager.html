What was done. Tripleseat exposes no line items through its API, so every May event's invoice document was pulled and parsed. Each line follows the BEO's room header (KAMPER'S, Kampers Rooftop Lounge, Bar Rotunda, Conservatory), a line that names Kamper's or Bar Rotunda goes to that outlet regardless, and standalone rooftop and Rotunda events stay 100% with their outlet. Service charge and admin fee come from each invoice's BILLING block and follow each outlet's share of base. Sales tax and gratuity are excluded throughout. Le Suprême and HIROKI-SAN private dining rows are their own book and are excluded.

Anthology's May is $448,894 net, $368,527 before service charge and admin fee. Toast shows $434,578 for Anthology, $14,316 less, and $122,369 of Kamper's A/R, $14,757 more than the BEO split gives it. The two offset almost exactly: Book Tower events total $556,506 here against $556,946 across both POS reports, a $440 difference. So the month's revenue is right and the question is only where the line between the outlets sits. Eleven of 22 Anthology event days tie within $700; the difference is five weddings where Kamper's rang more of the night than the BEO labels: Pierson 5/30 ($8,033), Buck 5/22 ($3,044), Lowery 5/02 ($2,740), Malewicz 5/23 ($2,411) and Yarosh 5/31 ($1,376). Running the other way, Toast Anthology is $7,415 above the BEO across Rachel's Network 5/04–06.

Kamper's takes more than 20% of a shared wedding. It carries the canapés, its $1,000/hour reception fee and the cocktail hour of the five-hour bar package. On the BEOs the eight May weddings that shared a room with Kamper's run 20–23%, averaging 21.9%; Toast says the true figure is higher, about 25%, once the five weddings above are counted where they were rung. Jacques & Kazoleas ran its cocktail hour at Bar Rotunda instead and splits 12% that way. Where the cocktail hour was booked as its own event (Lake, Malewicz, Yarosh) Kamper's share is carried in the standalone line.

BEO fixes at source. Lowery (5/02), Miller (5/29) and Pierson (5/30) bill a Kamper's cocktail hour but leave the canapé block unlabelled, the same omission Toast confirmed on Hannigan and Joseph; the cocktail-hour rule moves those canapés to Kamper's here, and Toast says Pierson's rooftop share was larger still. Roth Capital (5/12) and Buzz Solutions (5/13) carry "Corporate Events" as their area on the invoice although they ran on the rooftop and at Bar Rotunda. Rachel's Network Day 1 (5/04) lists Kampers Rooftop Bar among its rooms with no Kamper's lines; it stays with Anthology.
